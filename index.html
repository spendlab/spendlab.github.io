How to split real-world expenses

Three everyday situations, with a clear record of who paid and who shares the cost.

Only two people shared this cost

Choose who took part in each expense. A group trip can include costs shared by different people.

  1. Add the expense to your event and record who paid.
  2. Select only the members who share this expense. Split equally, use ratios or enter individual amounts.
  3. Review the event balances after adding the rest of your costs.

Example

Four friends are traveling together. Only two share a cost of 60. With an equal split, those two share 30 each; the other two share 0 of this expense.

Different people paid along the way

Record each payment and each member’s share as you go. SPEND brings them together to show each member’s balance.

  1. Create an event and add the people in your group.
  2. Record each expense with its payer and participants. If more than one person paid a single expense, record their contributions.
  3. Check member balances and suggested settlements, then share the result.

Example

Four people share 180 equally, so each person’s share is 45. A paid 120 and B paid 60. Their balances are A +75, B +15, C −45 and D −45. Suggested settlements show how to balance the record.

Keep travel costs in the local currency

Record what you actually spent in the local currency and review costs in your usual currency.

  1. Choose the currency when you record an expense.
  2. Enter an exchange rate or retrieve one in the app. Check the amount and rate used for your record.
  3. Review the converted amounts and group balances in your chosen currency.

Keep the rate clear

If a trip includes more than one currency, keep each expense’s original currency and review its conversion. Exchange rates can change, so check the rate used for each record.

A few things to know

Can each expense have different participants?

Choose the members who share each expense, then split equally, use ratios or enter individual amounts. You can record different participants for each cost within the same event.

Can I share expenses and settlement results?

Share expense details and settlement results so your group can review them together. Live sharing and sync are planned for future updates.

Can I keep a copy of my records?

You can export transactions as CSV to view them in Excel, and back up and restore all your records.
